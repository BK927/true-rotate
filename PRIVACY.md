# Privacy Policy

**TrueRotate** does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

TrueRotate runs entirely on your own computer. It does not connect to the
internet, contains no analytics or telemetry, and shows no ads. The only data it
stores is on your device:

- a settings file (your hotkeys and preferences) at
  `%AppData%\TrueRotate\config.json`, and
- a size-limited diagnostic log (rotation events and errors) at
  `%LocalAppData%\TrueRotate\logs\`.

Neither file ever leaves your computer. If you choose **Copy diagnostics** in
Settings, a report (app/Windows/GPU driver versions, monitor names and
orientations, your settings, and recent log lines) is copied to your clipboard so
you can paste it into a bug report yourself. The app never sends it anywhere.

Because TrueRotate collects no data, there is nothing for the developer to
access, use, or disclose to third parties.

If you have any questions, please open an issue at
<https://github.com/BK927/true-rotate/issues>.
